How this works (classic)

  1. Each bar is a growth factor (nutrient, light, etc.).
  2. Yield (pink line) sits at the shortest bar — surplus elsewhere does not raise it.
  3. Click a bar to add more of that input and see if yield moves.

How it works with AI

  1. Same logic for enterprise AI: firm-level value is capped by the scarcest input.
  2. Most firms are long on compute and short on human review.
  3. Only raising the shortest bar raises potential increase in value (the pink line).
