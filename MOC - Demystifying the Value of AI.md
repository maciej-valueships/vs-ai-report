# MOC — Demystifying the Value of AI

> **What this is.** The map-of-content for the Valueships AI value & pricing research project. Use this as the entry point. Every link points to a file in this folder. In Obsidian, the graph view will render this as the hub node.

> **Central thesis.** AI today delivers a 2x productivity factor on AI-fit tasks (not 10x). Macro CapEx of $725B+ in 2026 does not math against realised productivity gains of 1.5–2.9 % per year. Most enterprise AI pilots (95 % per MIT NANDA) produce no measurable P&L impact. The Valueships **Value Increase Index (VII)** collapses all sector evidence into one comparable percentage and drives a defensible pricing-model recommendation per sector.

## Methodology — proprietary brand stack

The dossier is structured exclusively around Valueships proprietary frameworks. Third-party pricing-strategy frameworks (Bain SaaS×AI quadrant, the Verticalist AI Services quadrant) have been deliberately removed as structuring lenses. External RCT evidence (Brynjolfsson, Dell'Acqua, Noy/Zhang, METR, Choi/Schwarcz) and macro econ research (Acemoglu, Goldman, McKinsey, Bain Tech Report 2025) remain as DATA INPUTS, not as competing frameworks.

The Valueships proprietary stack:

- **Valueships VII™ (Value Increase Index)** — the normalised value metric. Formula, methodology, and the live 17-sector benchmark calculator live in sheet `IDX01`.
- **Valueships VII Pricing Quadrant™** — the master 2×2 pricing-model decision framework (VII × Revenue Attribution). Sheet `VPQ01`.
- **Replaceability × Attribution Matrix** — finer 5×3 lens for sub-segment decisions. Sheet `PR01`.
- **SaaS Vertical Decision Tree** — 13 SaaS verticals mapped to recommended pricing model with a 5-step decision tree. Sheet `PR02`.
- **Value Decomposition (Cost vs Revenue)** — sheets `VI01`, `VI02`, `VI03`.
- **Vendor Capture Model** — sheets `V01`, `IDX02`.
- **10x Pricing Test (5 conditions)** — sheet `V02`.
- **Hype Tracker** — sheets `HY01`, `HY02`.
- **CapEx vs Productivity Math** — sheet `CX01`.
- **Industrial Revolutions Comparison** — sheet `HX01`.

The brand and methodology are proprietary to Valueships and may be cited as such in client work.
